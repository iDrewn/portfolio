Apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Seven iOS apps, written in SwiftUI: a news reader, tools for designers and doodlers, a cinema guide, a hockey app, a hotel guest app and a tech magazine app. Here is what each one does.

Leaf

An RSS reader where the news turns like a page.

  • iPhone
  • iPad
  • iPhone Duo
  • Svenska & English
  • SwiftUI · iOS 26+
  • Widget

Leaf is a news reader where every story is a page. Follow the sources you choose, swipe, and the page curls over like paper. On iPhone Duo and iPad two pages sit side by side, centred on the fold, and the stories keep clear of the crease when the device is part way open.

  • Suggested sources for your country, in sections, or add any feed or website
  • Reading place, sources and saved stories sync through iCloud, with no account
  • Notifications and a morning edition, made on the device with no server
  • Search, unread only, widget, OPML import and export, and no tracking

Framedevice

Screenshot and video mockups, and device info.

  • iPhone
  • iPad
  • Mac
  • iPhone Duo
  • SwiftUI · iOS 26+

Framedevice puts your screenshots and screen recordings in iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac frames, drawn as vectors with no Apple artwork. Put up to four devices on one canvas, pick a backdrop, shadow and caption, and export as images or MP4 video, in one size or several at once for the App Store and social media.

  • Share extension: send pictures straight from the share sheet
  • Saved looks sync between devices through iCloud, with no account
  • Backdrops from the screenshot's own colours, callouts and motion
  • Runs natively on the Mac as a Catalyst app

Just Draw

A blank page, eight colours and your finger.

  • iPhone
  • iPad
  • iPhone Duo
  • SwiftUI · iOS 27+

A simple drawing app: pick a colour, pick a brush size, draw, share. The canvas fills the screen and the controls are system toolbar items, so it feels at home on every device, including iPhone Duo.

  • Undo and redo through the system, including shake to undo
  • Works with finger or Apple Pencil
  • Your drawing is saved as you go
  • English and Swedish, and no tracking

Biokoll

What's on at your local cinema, and what's coming.

  • iPhone
  • Svenska & English
  • SwiftUI · iOS 26+
  • Liquid Glass

Biokoll shows the programme at five cinemas in southern Sweden: a poster grid of what's playing, premieres by month, and a trailer that plays right on the movie page. Tap a showtime to go straight to booking.

  • Filter by day and age rating
  • Saved for offline, with a banner that says when it was last updated
  • Reads the cinemas' own public data, no accounts and no tracking

Puckkoll

Your team in the SHL, SDHL or HockeyAllsvenskan, with the score as it changes.

  • iPhone
  • Apple Watch
  • iPhone Duo
  • Svenska & English
  • SwiftUI · iOS 26+
  • Widgets
  • Live Activities

Puckkoll follows the SHL, the SDHL and HockeyAllsvenskan: the schedule, live games with goals, line-ups and shots, tables and season statistics. A small server I run sends goal notifications and keeps a Live Activity on the Lock Screen, and there is a widget and an Apple Watch app. Tipset lets friends and family tip the results in groups.

  • Every club is a puck in its own colours, or a picture you choose yourself
  • Notifications per club, an optional delay for games you watch on TV, and a spoiler-free mode
  • Game recaps written on the iPhone with Apple Intelligence
  • Reads the leagues' own public pages, with no accounts, no ads and no tracking

Ekebacken

The official app for Ekebacken Hotell & Konferens in Markaryd.

  • iPhone
  • iPad
  • Svenska
  • SwiftUI · iOS 26+
  • Widget
  • On the App Store

The hotel's guest app, rebuilt for iOS 26 as version 3.0. See what's on month by month, check the lunch menu, book a room and get in touch. Upcoming events come from a WordPress plugin I wrote for the hotel's site, and new events can send a push notification.

  • Events by month, with a map and an add-to-calendar button
  • Lunch menu, room booking, and a contact form with table requests
  • Home screen widget with the next event, and push notifications
  • Works offline, with no accounts and no tracking

Teknikveckan

Articles, reviews and the Teknikbubblan forum in one app.

  • iPhone
  • iPad
  • iPhone Duo
  • Svenska
  • SwiftUI · iOS 26+
  • Liquid Glass

A native client for teknikveckan.se and its Discourse forum. The home screen shows the latest articles and reviews in a dark mobile design, and the forum sits one tab over, so you can read an article and jump straight into its discussion.

  • Review boxes with pros, cons, details and a rating, plus a reading list and search
  • The whole forum: topics, categories, threads, likes, replies, new topics and private messages
  • Links to articles and forum threads open inside the app
  • Anonymous statistics that can be switched off, and alternate app icons
