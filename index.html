RSS reader · iPhone · iPad · iPhone Duo

Leaf

An RSS reader where the news turns like a page.

Pages that turn

Two pages on the Duo.

Every story is a page. On iPhone Duo and iPad two pages sit side by side, centred on the fold, and the stories keep clear of the crease.

iCloud · Widget · No tracking

Yours, everywhere.

Sources, saved stories and reading place sync through iCloud with no account. Notifications and a morning edition are made on the device.

Mockups · iPhone · iPad · Mac

Framedevice

Mockups for screenshots and screen recordings, and everything about your device.

Now on the Mac

Up to four devices, one canvas.

Own vector frames for iPhone 16, 17 and 18, iPhone Duo, iPad, Apple Watch and MacBook, with backdrops, callouts, motion and video export.

Made for iPhone Duo

Every size in one go.

App Store and social sizes rendered into one folder, as PNG, JPEG, or an MP4 when a device has a screen recording.

Drawing · iPhone · iPad · iPhone Duo

Just Draw

A blank page, eight colors and your finger.

Small on purpose

Undo anything.

Shake to undo, swipe with three fingers or press ⌘Z. Clearing the page can be undone too. No accounts, no permissions and no network.

On every screen

Made for iPhone Duo.

The page fills whichever display you use, closed, open or part way.

Cinema guide · iPhone

Biokoll

What’s on at your local cinema, and what’s coming.

Trailers and tickets

Showtimes, plot, trailer.

A poster grid of what’s playing, premieres by month, and a trailer that plays right on the movie page. Tap a time to go straight to booking.

Offline · Private

Saved for later.

The schedule is kept for offline use, with a banner that says when it was updated. It reads the cinemas’ own public data: no accounts, no tracking.

Hockey · iPhone · Apple Watch · Duo

Puckkoll

Your team in the SHL, SDHL and HockeyAllsvenskan.

Match page

Every shot, on the map.

Live scores, pressure charts, shot maps and a summary written on your iPhone, for every game.

Alerts · Tipset

A notification when it counts.

Goal notifications, Live Activities, a widget, an Apple Watch app and Tipset to tip with friends. No accounts, no tracking.

Events

What’s on, month by month.

On the App Store

Lunch, rooms, contact.
